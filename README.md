# Acts 4:13 Planning Workspace

This workspace turns the foundational brand and website plans into an executable marketing and content system.

## Read in this order

1. **ACTS413-MARKETING-PLAN-v0.2.md**  
   Positioning, audiences, relationship ladder, launch strategy, channels, measurement, and trust boundaries.

2. **ACTS413-YEAR-ONE-CONTENT-PLAN-v0.1.md**  
   August 2026–July 2027 formation arc, content types, cadence, monthly plans, twelve giveaways, Dust and Chronicle release runways, future-book development, production workflow, governance, and decisions.

3. **ACTS413-52-WEEK-CONTENT-CALENDAR.csv**  
   One operating row for every week of Year One, including the anchor, room, giveaway, email action, community action, product milestone, and learning signal.

4. **ACTS413-CONTENT-INVENTORY-TEMPLATE.csv**  
   The schema for tracking every story, reflection, prayer, practice, excerpt, guide, video, product asset, permission, and derivative.

## Current planning assumptions

- Year One starts August 2026.
- Dust targets October 2026 only if its release gate passes.
- Chronicle targets bounded early access in June 2027 and a possible public release in July 2027 only if its product and trust gates pass.
- A second book is researched and concept-tested during Year One; no publication date is promised.
- The standard cadence is one strong weekly source package, one monthly giveaway, and one quarterly guided journey.

These are planning assumptions, not public commitments. Founder review should replace them with confirmed product status, capacity, budget, and priorities.
